# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Welcome: be kind, assume good faith, critique work not people, state
evidence for claims (that's the house style here — for conduct as much as
for code), and respect that maintainers may simply say no.

Unacceptable: harassment or insults; publishing others' private
information (emails, addresses, account details, subscriber data);
sexualized language or imagery; trolling or sustained disruption; and
submitting knowingly false "verified" results — in this project that's a
conduct issue, not just a quality one.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to the maintainers at start@axiovexsystems.com. Reports
are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove
content, warn, or ban participants whose behavior violates this code,
at their discretion, in proportion to the violation.
